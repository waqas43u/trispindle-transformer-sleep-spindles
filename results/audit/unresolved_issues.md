# Unresolved evidence limitations

1. **Historical and final MASS runs differ.** The previous Results section used approximately 81.0%, 79.2%, and 78.5% strict F1 for Conformer, Sparse-Local, and Global models. The retained checkpoint-linked runs yield 79.58%, 77.93%, and 64.56%. The historical manuscript also described a 4.59-M-parameter Global model, whereas the retained Global checkpoint has 2,776,800 parameters. They are therefore different model artifacts. The Global local directory is a resolved archive alias: its internal configuration names `results_transformer_mass_xai_5fold`, matching the DREAMS source root. All 30 checkpoint records agree in byte size, configuration, calibrator, decoder, ensemble weights, and metric-linked decoder values. The DREAMS manifests did not retain checkpoint hashes or copies, so exact cross-archive binary identity cannot be proved cryptographically after the fact.
2. **No final MASS F1@IoU 0.30 output was found.** This metric was omitted instead of interpolated or reconstructed.
3. **DREAMS boundary arrays were reconstructed post hoc.** Match arrays were not retained, but the original annotations, preserved predicted events, and authoritative greedy matcher remained available. A read-only reconstruction reproduced all 900 saved count/F1 rows within $1.11\times10^{-16}$ before boundary errors were calculated. Boundary summaries remain conditional on that matching rule and threshold.
4. **DREAMS folds are dependent.** The five MASS fold ensembles evaluated the same DREAMS excerpts and share training subjects. Their dispersion is descriptive and was not treated as independent replication.
5. **Cross-dataset aggregation differs.** MASS values are means across held-out subject folds under N2-only scoring; DREAMS values are excerpt-macro or event-micro summaries under audited mixed-stage coverage. Differences are labelled cross-protocol descriptive gaps.
6. **Runtime is not comparable.** Preserved logs do not establish uniform server load, epoch count, I/O conditions, or exclusive GPU use. Historical runtime values were removed.
7. **Explainability is internal only.** Complete Gradient SHAP, SmoothGrad, and frozen ablation artifacts were found for held-out MASS subjects. No DREAMS explainability claims are made.
8. **Perturbation is not causal.** Input representations are correlated transforms of the same C3 EEG. Fold-specific training-median replacement losses quantify conditional reliance of the frozen model, not physiological necessity.
9. **Numerical-equivalence audit artifact not found in result directories.** The DREAMS archive contains scoring-coverage and checkpoint-validation manifests, but not a standalone resampling/window-reconstruction numerical-equivalence report. This does not alter saved results, but it should be archived with the reproducibility package if available elsewhere.

No original experiment, checkpoint, probability file, annotation, or configuration was modified by the reconstruction script.
